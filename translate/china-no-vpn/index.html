Translate in China without a VPN

Google Translate's servers are blocked behind the Great Firewall, so the app that works everywhere else goes dark the moment you land. Tova runs translation on your device, so it keeps working without a VPN and without signal.

Common questions

About this guide

Why doesn't Google Translate work in China?

Google's translation servers are blocked by the Great Firewall, so the app can't reach them without a VPN. Tova avoids the problem by running translation on your phone instead of a blocked server.

Do I need to download anything before I fly?

Download Tova before you go so the on-device models are in place. After that the camera, voice, and text translation keep working with no connection.

Is a VPN ever required for Tova?

No. Tova is built so the core translation never depends on a server you can't reach inside China. There is nothing to configure.
