Korea for gamers: PC bangs, LoL Park and game shops

Last updated: November 2026

Korea plays its games in PC bangs, watches them at LoL Park, and hosts the League of Legends World Championship again in 2027. Here is how to do all of it as a visitor, without a Korean phone number.

Play at a PC bang

A PC bang (PC방) is Korea’s version of a gaming café: rows of fast PCs with good chairs and headsets, often open around the clock, with food you order from the screen and staff bring to your seat. They are everywhere, and they are where most Koreans play.

You don’t need a Korean phone number. At the kiosk, choose non-member (비회원), pay by cash or card, and take the number it gives you. Find a free seat on the seat map and type the number in to log on. Membership gets lower rates and keeps unused time, but it needs Korean phone verification. Smoking is banned in PC bangs.

Prices vary by place and time of day. The team-branded PC bangs below cost more than a neighbourhood one, and come with merchandise, lounges and food.

No non-member button on the kiosk? Show the counter this: 비회원으로 이용할게요 (“I’ll use it as a non-member”).

PC bangs run by pro teams

  • T1 Base Camp Gangnam, two minutes from Sinnonhyeon Station: a 24-hour PC zone, the T1 flagship store and a trophy room. Hotels near Sinnonhyeon.
  • Gen.G GGX in Dongdaemun: 252 PCs and a Gen.G store with items sold only here.
  • RedForce PC bang in Jonggak, about 200 seats, linked underground to LoL Park.
  • T1 Base Camp Busan in Haeundae: a PC café, T1 Shop and esports pub.

Gaming hotel rooms are rare

Unlike China, Korea has no big esports-hotel scene. Trip.com has esports pages for Seoul and Busan, but when we checked them on 20 November 2026 most listings were ordinary hotels; only a handful listed a gaming computer or gaming chair in their facilities.

If you want a room with a gaming PC, look for “Gaming computer” in the facilities and check the room photos before you book. Otherwise stay near a PC bang; they are easy to find in any busy neighbourhood.

Watch the LCK at LoL Park

LoL Park (롤파크) is the home of the LCK, Korea’s League of Legends league. All ten teams play here, in an arena of about 450 seats that wraps around the stage, so you sit a few metres from the players. It is on the third floor of Gran Seoul at 33 Jong-ro, Jonggak Station Exit 1, with a Riot Store, a PC café and a café in the same space.

Overseas fans buy tickets on NOL World (world.nol.com), in English. Korean residents use NOL Ticket. Big matches, especially T1’s, sell out in seconds, so be logged in and ready when sales open. Bring your passport to the match.

Hotels near LoL Park put you a short walk from Jonggak Station.

Stay near the big venues

KSPO Dome (올림픽체조경기장) in Olympic Park held the 2026 LCK finals. Hotels near KSPO Dome.

BEXCO (벡스코) in Busan hosts G-STAR, Korea’s biggest game show, every November, and held the 2026 Overwatch Asia finals during the show. Hotels near BEXCO.

KINTEX (킨텍스) in Goyang, north-west of Seoul, hosts AGF and PlayX4, and has held StarCraft finals. Hotels near KINTEX.

Worlds 2027 comes to Korea: the quarterfinals and semifinals at Suwon Convention Center (수원컨벤션센터), and the final at Seoul Arena (서울아레나), a new arena being built in Chang-dong. Hotels in Suwon.

Before you play online

  • Korean phone verification (본인인증) is what most often stops visitors on Korean websites and apps. Naver now lets foreigners verify with a passport for bookings and payments through Naver.
  • Your own game accounts usually work from Korea. Making a new account on a Korean server can ask for Korean phone verification, so set up anything you need before you travel.
  • T-money cards pay for buses, the subway, taxis and convenience stores.
  • PC bangs take cash or card from non-members, so you don’t need a Korean payment app to play.

Common questions

About this guide

Can foreigners use PC bangs in Korea?

Yes. Choose non-member (비회원) at the kiosk, pay by cash or card, and log on at a free seat with the number it gives you. You don’t need a Korean phone number; that is only needed for membership.

How do I buy LCK tickets as a foreigner?

Use NOL World (world.nol.com), the English version of the NOL ticket site, which sells LCK matches at LoL Park to overseas fans. Big matches sell out in seconds, so be logged in when sales open.

Where is Worlds 2027 being held?

In Korea. Riot has announced the quarterfinals and semifinals at Suwon Convention Center and the final at Seoul Arena, a new arena in Chang-dong, Seoul. The first stage is in Nagoya, Japan. Riot has not announced the dates yet.

Where can I buy Nintendo and Pokémon goods in Seoul?

Dopamine Station on the 3rd floor of I’Park Mall at Yongsan Station has Korea’s biggest Nintendo shop and a PlayStation store. Korea has no permanent Pokémon Center; Pokémon shops in Seoul are pop-ups that come and go.
