Why we built this

Google Translate doesn't work in China. So we built one that does.

Hi Product Hunt 👋 — I built Tova after a trip to Shanghai where I spent twenty minutes watching the Google Translate spinner do nothing through the Great Firewall, then finally just pointed at a menu and guessed. The dish was great. The experience was not.

Every translation app I tried had the same blind spot: the moment you crossed a border into a country with strict network rules, the app you'd been depending on quietly stopped working. The fix everyone recommends — "just use a VPN" — is exactly the kind of friction nobody wants on day one of a trip.

Tova was built around the opposite assumption: the network is unreliable, so the app shouldn't be. When the backend is unreachable, on-device neural translation kicks in automatically (Apple Translation on iOS, ML Kit on Android). Voice transcription runs locally on a Whisper Tiny model — your audio never leaves the phone. Camera OCR works across 11 script families. All 118 target languages are unlocked for free.

Pro and Pro Plus add the things travelers and business users ask for after the first week — favourites, offline phrase packs, glossary, non-Google engines (Baidu, DeepL, OpenAI), boardroom 3-way voice, document OCR, PDF upload, and a medical/legal domain pack. Pricing is transparent and listed below — no dark patterns, no email walls.

I'd love feedback on what's missing, what's broken, and what you wish translation apps did differently. I read every comment.
