Accuracy

Where the readings come from

Jyutping tools usually ship one reading per character, which gets polyphones wrong in exactly the words people look up. 行 is hang4 on its own but hong4 in 銀行. 樂 is lok6 on its own but ngok6 in 音樂.

This tool checks whole words first and only falls back to single characters when no word matches, so 銀行 comes back ngan4 hong4 and 音樂 comes back jam1 ngok6.

Where a character genuinely has several readings and no word decides between them, turning on alternate readings shows the others underneath instead of hiding the ambiguity.

Coverage is roughly 32,000 characters and 20,000 disambiguated words, including the colloquial Cantonese characters that dictionaries built for Mandarin tend to miss.

Common questions

About this tool

Is this Jyutping converter free?

Yes. It is free to use, there is no sign-up, and there is no limit on how many times you can convert text.

Does my text get uploaded anywhere?

No. The dictionary is downloaded to your browser once and the conversion runs on your device. The Cantonese you paste is never sent to a server.

What do the numbers after each syllable mean?

They are tones. Cantonese has six, numbered 1 to 6, and Jyutping writes them as digits rather than accent marks — hou2 is tone 2. You can hide them with the tone-number switch, but two words can differ only by tone, so keeping them on is usually what you want.

Does it handle characters with more than one reading?

Yes. Whole words are matched before single characters, so 銀行 is read ngan4 hong4 rather than ngan4 hang4. When nothing disambiguates a character, switch on alternate readings to see the other possibilities.

Does it know colloquial Cantonese characters?

Yes. Written Cantonese characters such as 嘅, 咗, 喺, 唔, 佢, 嘢 and 冇 are covered, which is where dictionaries built for Mandarin usually stop.

Does it work with simplified characters?

Yes, though Cantonese is normally written in traditional characters. Both are covered.

What is the difference between Jyutping and pinyin?

Jyutping romanizes Cantonese and marks six tones with numbers. Pinyin romanizes Mandarin and marks four tones with accents. The same characters read differently — 你好 is nei5 hou2 in Cantonese and nǐ hǎo in Mandarin. Use the pinyin converter for Mandarin.

How much text can I convert at once?

Up to 5,000 characters per conversion. Paste longer documents in sections.
