The three-touch day

A daily drill, not a lecture. Three short touch points keep the loop tight enough to maintain a streak but light enough that you'll actually do it.

7am · Wake

One character.

A daily reminder brings you the day's character. Trace every stroke in the right direction, in the right order — that's it. ~90 seconds.

Mid-day · Sprint

Say it out loud.

A short AI conversation using the character in context. On-device Whisper transcribes you — pronunciation scored, not just passed.

Evening · Listen

Hear it in the wild.

A 30-second clip of a native speaker using the character — a movie line, a song lyric, a street sign. Passive, no quiz.

How a drill actually looks

Real screens from the app. Stroke validation uses Make Me a Hanzi median paths. Pronunciation scoring runs on-device via Whisper Tiny — no audio leaves your phone.

Common questions

Honest answers about what ships at v0.1 and what's still coming.

Is there an alarm, and does it replace my iOS alarm clock?

It's a daily reminder, not an alarm. Pick a time in Settings and your Tova Learn app sends a local notification; tap it to open that day's character. Apple doesn't let apps replace the built-in alarm clock, so this is a gentle scheduled nudge — not a loud alarm sound.

How does the pronunciation scoring work?

OpenAI Whisper Tiny runs on your device — Apple Neural Engine. Your microphone audio never leaves your phone. The transcript is matched against the target phrase character-by-character to produce a score.

Where does each course start?

Tova Learn Chinese opens with HSK 1 — 150 foundational characters, about 150 days of daily content. Tova Learn Japanese opens with Hiragana and Katakana before moving into JLPT N5 kanji. Each level is enough to validate the loop before expanding; HSK 2-6 and higher JLPT levels are queued as Pro content drops, and custom phrase packs from your Tova Translate Favourites also fill the queue.

Is Tova Learn available for Japanese and Korean?

Tova Learn Japanese is live on the App Store now (Hiragana, Katakana & JLPT kanji), alongside Tova Learn Chinese. Tova Learn Korean (Hangul, TOPIK) is planned. Each is a separate app under the Tova Learn family, sharing the same trace-and-speak engine and on-device Whisper pronunciation scoring.

What's the connection to Tova Translate?

Same team, same brand family. Your saved Favourites in Tova Translate become a study queue in the matching Tova Learn app automatically when both are installed. The "Learn this" button on a Chinese, Japanese or Korean result card deep-links straight into a drill.
