Common questions

Honest answers about what ships at v0.1 and what's still coming.

Does the alarm actually replace the iOS alarm clock?

No — Apple doesn't expose a public API to replace the system alarm. Tova Learn Chinese fires a local notification at your chosen time and plays a sound; the daily-drill flow opens when you tap it. Same pattern Alarmy and similar apps use.

How does the pronunciation scoring work?

OpenAI Whisper Tiny runs on your device — Apple Neural Engine. Your microphone audio never leaves your phone. The transcript is matched against the target phrase character-by-character to produce a score.

Why only HSK 1 at launch?

HSK 1 is 150 characters — about 150 days of daily content, which is enough to validate the loop before expanding. HSK 2-6 are queued as a Pro content drop. Custom phrase packs from your Tova Translate Favourites also fill the queue.

Will there be a Tova Learn Japanese or Korean?

Yes — Japanese kanji (with KanjiVG) and Korean Hangul are planned as separate apps under the Tova Learn family, sharing the same Whisper backbone and brand. Mandarin first to nail the loop.

What's the connection to Tova Translate?

Same team, same brand family. Tova Translate's saved Favourites become Tova Learn Chinese's content queue automatically when both apps are installed. The "Learn this" button on Tova Translate result cards deep-links into a Tova Learn Chinese drill.
