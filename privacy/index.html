Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

This policy explains what data Tova通话 collects, why, and how you can control it. Tova通话 is published by the developer operating under the contact email listed below; "we", "us", and "our" refer to that same entity.

1. Data we collect

  • Text you ask us to translate, plus the source and target language codes. This is sent over TLS to our backend, which forwards it to the translation engine you have selected (Google Translate by default, optionally DeepL, OpenAI, or Baidu on Pro). Translation text is never written to a database, a log or disk, and is not used to train models. To answer repeat requests faster, a finished translation may be held in our server's memory for up to 7 days, keyed by a one-way hash and not linked to you or your device; it is cleared whenever the server restarts.
  • OCR results. Images captured by the camera are processed on-device using Apple VisionKit and Google ML Kit. Only the recognised text string is transmitted to the backend; the image itself never leaves your device.
  • Voice input. When you use the Speak tab, transcription happens entirely on-device via Apple Speech (manual mode), Android SpeechRecognizer (manual mode on Android), or OpenAI Whisper running locally on your device (auto-detect mode, both platforms). Audio is never transmitted to our servers, to OpenAI, or to any third party — only the recognised text string is sent to the translation backend, the same way typed text is. The Whisper model file (~75 MB) is downloaded once on first use of auto-detect mode from the public Hugging Face mirror; Hugging Face sees your IP address and the public model identifier but no Tova-specific account or device identifier.
  • App settings, favourites, glossary, and history. Stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences and SQLite. Never transmitted, except that glossary entries can be synced between your own iCloud-linked devices via Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore (iCloud Key-Value Store).
  • Apple In-App Purchase receipts. Receipts are sent to our backend only for server-side verification, per Apple guidelines. No card data is ever seen by us.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, a signature (error message + top 8 stack frames) and light environment data (OS version, UI language, active service, Pro status, last screen) are sent to our backend for diagnostics. No personal identifiers and no message content.
  • Usage events (Tova Learn apps). Named events recording that something happened — a drill was finished, a review question was answered correctly or not, an audio button was tapped — together with an install identifier the app generates at random on your device. This identifier is not your Apple ID, not your advertising identifier, and is not linked to any account; deleting the app discards it. We use these counts to see which parts of the app actually help people learn. No text you type and no personal identifiers are included. Retained for up to 120 days.
  • Feedback you submit in-app. The message body, the email address you provide, and light environment data (OS, active language, Pro status). Used to reply to you and triage issues.
  • Advertising identifiers (free tier only). On iOS, if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission, the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google AdMob to deliver personalised ads. On Android, the Google Advertising ID (AAID) is shared with Google AdMob by default unless you delete it in Settings → Google → Ads. If tracking is denied or the AAID is deleted, only non-personalised ads are served.
  • EU / UK / Switzerland advertising consent (free tier only). Before any ad SDK initialises, users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are presented with a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your selection is stored on-device by the SDK and determines whether AdMob is permitted to load. If you decline or no consent is given, no ads are shown.
  • Basic usage events. The app reports a small number of named events to our own server — that the app was launched, and whether an ad slot was requested, filled, or failed to fill (with the ad network's numeric error code). Each event carries the app version, platform, OS version, and device locale. They exist so we can tell whether an advertising or reliability problem is affecting real users. They are not shared with any advertising network, analytics vendor, or data broker, and are never used to build an advertising profile.
  • An install identifier. Usage events above carry a random string the app generates for itself on first launch and stores on your device. It lets us tell "one person opened the app ten times" from "ten people opened it once". It is not the advertising identifier, not the device serial or vendor ID, and is not linked to your name, email, or Apple account. Uninstalling the app discards it permanently; a reinstall generates a new, unrelated one.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name or contact details, unless you voluntarily submit them via in-app feedback or email us directly.
  • Precise location. We do not request location permission.
  • Camera roll contents beyond the specific image you choose to translate.
  • Health, financial, or biometric data.

3. Third parties

  • Translation engines. Text you translate is routed through the engine you select: Google Translate, DeepL, OpenAI, or Baidu. Their respective privacy policies apply to text routed through them.
  • Apple. For In-App Purchase, Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text, VisionKit, the Translation framework, iCloud Key-Value Store, and the App Store.
  • OpenAI Whisper (open-source model). When you enable the auto-detect language toggle on the Speak tab, Tova uses an on-device Whisper model to transcribe your voice. Audio is processed entirely on your device; no voice data is sent to OpenAI, Hugging Face, Tova, or any other server.
  • Hugging Face. Hosts the public Whisper model file that Tova downloads once on first use of auto-detect mode. Hugging Face sees your IP address and the public model identifier; no Tova-specific account or identifier is shared.
  • Google AdMob (free tier only). For serving advertising. AdMob may collect the data described in Apple's ATT prompt if you grant permission.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Used to obtain GDPR / e-Privacy consent for advertising in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland before any ad SDK initialises. Your consent selection is stored on-device.
  • Gmail SMTP for transactional email (your feedback receipts and internal support notifications).
  • We do not share data with data brokers.

4. Retention

Translation requests are not stored: a finished translation may stay in the server's memory cache for up to 7 days (see section 1), and is never written to disk. Feedback entries you submit and crash reports are retained for up to 24 months for triage and product-quality purposes, then deleted. Usage events are retained for 120 days, then deleted automatically. Favourites, glossary terms, and scan history live exclusively on your device; uninstalling the app removes them.

If both Tova Translate and Tova Learn Chinese are installed on the same device, your saved favourites involving Chinese are shared between the two apps via an iOS App Group container — entirely on-device, never sent to a server. Uninstalling either app removes its access to the shared container; uninstalling both removes the shared data.

6. Your rights & privacy choices

You can exercise these privacy choices at any time:

  • Opt out of advertising tracking (iOS). Tova requests App Tracking Transparency permission for the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) on the free tier. Decline the prompt at first launch, or revoke at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle Tova通话 off. With tracking denied, no IDFA is shared with Google AdMob; only non-personalised ads are served.
  • Opt out of advertising tracking (Android). On Android, the Google Advertising ID (AAID) is the equivalent identifier used by AdMob. Open Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads (some manufacturers use Settings → Privacy → Ads) and toggle Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization. With the AAID deleted, AdMob can no longer link ad requests back to your device; only non-personalised ads are served. EU / UK / Switzerland users are additionally shown Google's User Messaging Platform consent form on first launch and can update their choices at any time from within Tova's Settings → Privacy & Ads → Ad privacy choices (from version 1.2.4; the entry appears only where this consent applies).
  • Reset your advertising identifier. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Reset Advertising Identifier. Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID. Either generates a new identifier and severs prior ad-attribution links.
  • Remove ads entirely. Pro and Team subscriptions disable Google AdMob on both iOS and Android; no advertising identifier is collected or shared on those tiers.
  • Delete or access your server-side data. Email support@tovatranslate.app with subject "Delete my data" or "Data access request" and we will respond within 30 days. Include the email address you used to submit feedback so we can locate your records.
  • Delete all local data. Uninstalling the app removes favourites, glossary, scan history, and cached offline phrase packs. iOS users: iCloud-synced glossary entries clear when you uninstall on every linked device or disable iCloud Key-Value Storage for Tova. Android users: Tova does not currently sync any user data to a Google account; uninstalling the app removes all local data immediately.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, the right to correct inaccuracies, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Tova does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Sharing the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) with Google AdMob to serve personalised ads may be classified as "sharing" under CPRA — you can opt out by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS) or deleting the advertising ID (Android, see the bullet above), revoking tracking in system Settings, or upgrading to a paid tier (which disables AdMob entirely on both platforms). We do not discriminate against users who exercise these rights.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR / UK-GDPR). In addition to the rights above, you have the right to data portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Our lawful bases for processing are: contract performance (translation requests, IAP receipts), legitimate interest (crash diagnostics, feedback triage, security), and consent (advertising tracking, managed via Google's User Messaging Platform).

Our backend and its third-party services operate in the United States and the European Union. By using Tova通话 you consent to the transfer of the limited data described above to those jurisdictions.
